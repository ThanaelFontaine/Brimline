# Brimline 0.2.2

Brimline 0.2.2 fixes the hiding of menu bar icons when Brimline's bar sits far right, and makes the gauge bolder on a regular screen. It is still private: the repository and the site are not public yet, so the links below work once they are.

## What is new

- **Hiding works with the bar far right.** Brimline used to refuse with "The bar is too far right to hide reliably" as soon as the menu bar held many icons. It now always hides what it can. macOS lets the bar push the icons by about half the screen, so when the bar is far right, the icons nearest it may stay visible (more of them with an app whose menus are short); Brimline then says so, and how to fix it: keep only the icons to hide left of the bar, and drag the others to its right with Command held down.
- **A bolder gauge on a regular screen**: the same look as on a Retina screen.

## Good to know

- Hiding still uses a technique Apple does not document, tried on macOS 27.0.1 on a 1920 pt screen without a notch. See [docs/menu-bar.md](../menu-bar.md).
- Brimline is not notarized by Apple. A disk image downloaded in a browser needs **Open Anyway** once, in System Settings, Privacy & Security ([Apple's page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445)).
