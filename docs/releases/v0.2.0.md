# Brimline 0.2.0

Second version of Brimline, the macOS menu bar app (formerly Sightglass) that shows how much of your AI limits each of your accounts has used. It is private: the repository and the site are not public yet, nothing is announced.

## What works

- **Five providers**, each off until you turn it on (Settings, Accounts, or `brimline providers enable <name>`): Claude (through Claude Code), ChatGPT (through Codex), Gemini (through Antigravity), Grok (through the Grok CLI) and Cursor. Each was tried on a real account on 27 October 2026, on the plan named in [docs/providers.md](../providers.md) and nowhere else.
- **Several Claude accounts**, also those with no folder on the Mac, through what Claude Code reports on its status line or in stream-json (`brimline report claude`): no token read, no request, no quota spent.
- **A gauge in the menu bar**: the glyph lies down, with one part per account in the color of that account, and a red "!" from 90 % on one account. The percentage next to it, the tooltip and VoiceOver follow.
- **A color for each account**, kept in the settings: ten colors, a free color, warnings (never a refusal) when two accounts are hard to tell apart.
- **Tidy the menu bar** (Settings, Menu Bar): where to turn off the icons of Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor, and an optional "Hide without removing" that hides icons behind a thin bar of Brimline's own, with no permission asked.
- **For your agents**: `get_usage` and `best_account` take a `provider`; every account says its `provider`, `source` and, when it cannot be read, its `reason`. Two new states say it in words: `not_readable` and `format_changed`.
- **Renamed Brimline**, with a new icon and glyph. Your settings and cache are copied from the old folder at the first launch, nothing is deleted.
- **Safe reading**: closed list of URLs per provider, sign-in files opened without following a link, no refresh token read, nothing renewed, every text a provider sends filtered.

## What does not work yet, or was not tried

- **No paid plan was tried.** ChatGPT, Grok and Cursor were read on a free account only. xAI returns no credit limit for a free Grok account, so Brimline shows a sentence and no percentage. The comparison with the usage pages of OpenAI and Cursor is pending. Gemini was read through Antigravity CLI 1.4.8 and 1.4.9 (the percentage seen live at 100 % remaining only, the plan is not given by `agy`); another build launches nothing.
- **The reading of ChatGPT, Grok, Cursor and Claude is not documented by the providers**: it can change or stop without notice, and Brimline says so in words when it does.
- **Hiding icons** is a technique Apple does not document. It was tried on macOS 27.0.1, on a screen without a notch only: not on a MacBook with a notch, nor with several screens, nor across sleep and lock.
- **A Team or Enterprise plan** was not tried: Claude Code may give no `rate_limits` for such a seat, so its status line report may show no number. A Claude Code that keeps its sign-in outside the Keychain and the folder, a Codex that keeps it in the Keychain, or a token made with `claude setup-token` are not read.
- **MacBook Pro and macOS 14 (Sonoma)**: not tried yet.

## Install

See [docs/install.md](../install.md): `scripts/build_app.sh` then `scripts/install.sh`. The installer quits the old Sightglass app and prints the commands that move its old files to the Trash. Agents register the server again: `claude mcp add --scope user brimline -- ~/.local/bin/brimline mcp`.
